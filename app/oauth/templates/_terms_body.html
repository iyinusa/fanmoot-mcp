For using {{ public_base_url }} to connect an app or assistant to Fanmoot. Last updated {{ updated }}.

What this is

A service that lets apps and assistants read Fanmoot's football conversation and, if you connect your account, post and react on it as you. Your Fanmoot account itself, and everything you post, stays under fanmoot.com's own terms and community rules.

Connecting an app

  • An app can do only what you ticked when you connected it, and you can disconnect it at any time from connected apps.
  • What an app posts as you counts as you posting it. Read a take back before you let an assistant send it.
  • Many apps register themselves, and Fanmoot has not vetted them. Connect the ones you trust.

Fair use

Requests are rate-limited. An app or address that abuses the service may be slowed down, blocked or disconnected without notice. Do not use the service to get round anything fanmoot.com restricts.

No guarantees

The service is provided as it is. Scores and fixtures come from fanmoot.com and may be late or wrong. The service may be changed, paused or withdrawn.

Changes

If these terms change, the date above changes. Continuing to use a connected app after that means you accept the new version.
